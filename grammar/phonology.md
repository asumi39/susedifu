# Phonology

> Claim tags: **(attested)** = generalizable from lexicon.toml; **(proposed)** = new, needs owner confirmation; **open** = undecided — the owner will supply or decide.

## Phoneme inventory

- Vowels (attested): a e i o u — five vowels; no length or diphthong contrasts attested. Values follow the Japanese vowel set with one deviation (confirmed, 2026-11-08; u moved to the mainstream rounded value the same day): a [ä] open central, i [i], u [u] rounded — unlike the Japanese [ɯ], e [e̞], o [o̞]. Japanese-style allophony (vowel devoicing, consonant palatalization before i): open — not assumed.
- Consonants (attested): b d f g h k m r s t w y z
- h is a glottal fricative [h] (confirmed, 2026-11-08).
- r is a voiced alveolar tap [ɾ] — the light rhotic, the weak member of the r–j pair (confirmed, 2026-11-08); heavier trill realizations are avoided, as they would invert the strength order.
- j (attested, marginal): occurs only in the johua family (the loanword and its derivatives susejohua, dijohua). j is a voiced retroflex fricative [ʐ] — approximant realization [ɻ] acceptable; the sound of Mandarin pinyin r (confirmed, 2026-11-08), the strong member of the r–j pair.
- The precise phonetic values of the remaining consonants: open — do not assume them.

## Phonotactics

- Syllable template: (C)V — attested across all 68 forms.
- Words begin with a consonant and end with a vowel (attested: no vowel-initial or consonant-final form among the 68).
- No consonant clusters, no geminates, no vowel hiatus (attested: no counterexamples).
- j is restricted to the johua family (attested).
- Normative status: open — these are attested generalizations; whether they bind future coinages awaits owner confirmation.

## Prosody

- Stress: word-initial — every word stresses its first syllable (confirmed, 2026-11-08).
- Rationale: compounding is head-initial, so the semantic core is always the first element; initial stress puts the prominence on the meaning core (wozahosiwe ★WO-za-ho-si-we, mahagogafu ★MA-ha-go-ga-fu, susedifu ★SU-se-di-fu). In di- prefixed words the stress falls on the prefix, which itself carries the category meaning 'person'.
- Secondary stress in long words: open.

## Loanword adaptation

- l → d (attested twice): dati < 拉丁 lādīng, dodi < 蘿莉 luólì — l is absent from the inventory
- Nasal codas of the source are dropped: dati < lādīng (ding → ti), johua < Zhōnghuá (zhong → jo)
- Retroflex affricates soften to the voiced retroflex continuant (attested once): johua < Zhōnghuá — zh [ʈʂ] → j [ʐ]
- j exists only as loanword output (attested)
- Not yet covered by any rule: vowel-initial sources, non-nasal final consonants, source clusters and diphthongs — the first cases will set the precedents.
