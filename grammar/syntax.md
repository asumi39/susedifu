- sa is the copula (attested gloss). Its position relative to the predicate: open (probes 1, 8 assume it directly links subject and predicate).

## Noun phrase

- Attributive adjectives follow their head noun (confirmed, 2026-11-08; probes 4, 9).
- **de** = 'of': [N de X] links the noun to its possessor/source (confirmed, 2026-11-08; probes 3, 10). Example: yikawoza de disamu 'the staff of the king'.
- Demonstratives, numerals, quantifiers: open — none exist in the lexicon; slots are reserved in swadesh-coverage.md for the owner to fill.

## si and so

- **si / so — one preposition with voice-conditioned allomorphy** (confirmed, 2026-11-08): si in declarative clauses, so in passive clauses. What the preposition marks beyond voice, and where its phrase sits in the clause: open (probes 5, 6).
